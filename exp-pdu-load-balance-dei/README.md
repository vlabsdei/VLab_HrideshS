### About the Experiment

This experiment introduces students to the electrical power distribution challenges in modern data center racks. Students investigate how unbalanced phase currents, shifted phase angles, and harmonic distortion affect neutral return current, power delivery status, and breaker trip conditions in a three-phase Power Distribution Unit (PDU).

The experiment provides an interactive simulation of a three-phase PDU system where students can adjust individual phase currents and angles to observe the resulting neutral current, power delivery states (Underpowered, Optimal, Breaker Tripped), and the effects of harmonic distortion surges. The simulation demonstrates the importance of balanced power distribution for reliable and safe data center operation.
