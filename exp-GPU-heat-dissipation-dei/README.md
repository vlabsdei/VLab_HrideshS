### About the Experiment

This experiment introduces students to the thermal management challenges associated with modern Graphics Processing Units (GPUs) used in data centers and high-performance computing systems. Students investigate how power consumption, ambient temperature, thermal resistance, cooling efficiency, and thermal paste degradation influence GPU junction temperature and overall system performance.

The experiment provides an interactive visualization of the GPU cooling stack, including the GPU die, thermal interface material (thermal paste), heatsink, cooling fans, and PCB. Students can manipulate system parameters and observe their impact on heat transfer, cooling effectiveness, and thermal throttling behavior.

The experiment also demonstrates real-world failure scenarios such as thermal paste degradation and HVAC system failure, helping students understand the importance of thermal engineering in modern computing infrastructure.
