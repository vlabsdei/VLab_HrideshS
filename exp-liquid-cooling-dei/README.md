### About the Experiment

This experiment explores the thermal dynamics of liquid cooling systems used in modern GPU data centers and high-performance computing facilities. Students investigate how coolant mass flow rate, inlet water temperature, and GPU heat load influence outlet water temperature, thermal resistance, and the environmental water consumption penalty associated with large-scale cooling infrastructure.

The experiment provides an interactive simulation of a liquid cooling loop, including a cold plate, coolant pump, heat exchanger, and piping network. Students can manipulate system parameters and observe their impact on cooling performance, thermal resistance, and water consumption.

The experiment also highlights the environmental impact of data center cooling by demonstrating the extreme water consumption that occurs in modern computing facilities, and the engineering trade-offs between cooling performance, energy consumption, and sustainability.
