### About the Experiment

This experiment explores pipeline parallelism as a strategy for distributing deep neural network training across multiple GPUs. Students investigate how the number of model layers, pipeline stage splits, and micro-batch sizing affect pipeline bubble overhead, wall-clock training time, and per-device VRAM reduction. The experiment connects concepts from earlier experiments (thermal dissipation and interconnect performance) to demonstrate how compute cycles and transfer delays combine in pipeline-parallel architectures.
